Poetry

Records that
say what people can’t.

Elegies, a lyric sequence, and experimental poems that use non-literary records as form: a protein sequence that folds, memory files that corrupt, telemetry from two phones, the mathematics of attention. The texts are held back while they are under submission to journals.

Experimental

  • Spongiform Epistemologyverse with ASCII protein notation; misinformation misfolding through a family
  • Dear Dada letter interleaved with “verified” memory blocks that contradict and corrupt
  • Parallel Processingdevice telemetry around an event neither device records; also specified as a three-screen installation
  • Attention Was All You Neededa long meditation on attention as mechanism, market and love
